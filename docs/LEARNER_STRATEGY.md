# Learner Strategy: A1 to B1 Life German

## Learner Profile

Primary learner:

- Indian Telugu/English speaker
- German beginner
- goal is life, work, and relationship conversation
- Goethe exams are useful checkpoints, but not the main purpose

## Why German Feels Hard

The app should directly train the hardest gaps for a Telugu/English learner:

- gender and articles: der, die, das
- case changes: den, dem, einen, einem
- verb position and word order
- long compound words
- pronunciation and speaking confidence
- producing sentences quickly, not only recognizing them

## Best Path

The fastest useful path is not grammar-only and not chat-only.

Use this loop every day:

```text
1 tiny mission
1 weak word
1 tiny rule
1 recall task
1 spoken sentence
1 roleplay use
1 notebook memory
```

## Level Goals

### A1: Survival German

The learner can:

- introduce themselves
- ask simple questions
- shop, order food, ask price, ask size
- say what they need
- understand slow simple replies

Training focus:

- fixed sentence frames
- basic verbs
- articles in common phrases
- listen and repeat
- simple roleplays

### A2: Daily Life German

The learner can:

- make appointments
- talk about routine, travel, apartment, health, work basics
- write simple messages
- ask follow-up questions
- repair misunderstandings

Training focus:

- sentence expansion
- past tense basics
- modal verbs
- common connectors
- realistic daily roleplays

### B1: Usable Conversation

The learner can:

- explain problems
- give opinions
- tell short stories
- talk about work and plans
- maintain everyday conversations
- write practical emails/messages

Training focus:

- opinions and reasons
- job and social German
- longer roleplays
- correction memory
- speed and confidence

## Daily Experience

Mila should prescribe a small mission, not overwhelm the learner.

Example:

```text
Today we repair articles after suchen.
Weak word: der Pullover.
Tiny rule: after suchen, masculine der becomes einen.
Sentence: Ich suche einen Pullover in Groesse M.
Roleplay: Ask Lisa for a sweater.
Watch: avoid "eine Pullover".
```

For a first-time German learner, fewer choices are better. The app should decide the next mission and keep the learner moving. Free commands are powerful later, but the daily beginner flow should start with one obvious action: Start mission.

## What Success Feels Like

The learner opens the app and knows exactly what to do.

They do not browse content. They train.

They finish one loop and feel:

- I spoke today.
- I fixed one weakness.
- Mila remembers what I missed.
- Tomorrow will continue from my real mistakes.
